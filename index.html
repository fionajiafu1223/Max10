The Sedona Method

背后的方法The method behind it

Freed 基于圣多纳释放法——由莱斯特·雷文森创立、传承六十余年的释放练习。它释放的不只是当下的情绪，更是情绪底下长期积压的欲望、限制性信念与内在阻力。不分析、不对抗，只是问：我能欢迎它吗？我愿意放下它吗？什么时候？——现在。放下的部分，就永远地放下了。 Freed is based on the Sedona Method — a releasing practice founded by Lester Levenson over sixty years ago. It releases not just the feeling of the moment, but the wants, limiting beliefs, and inner resistance beneath it. No analyzing, no fighting — simply: could I welcome it? Could I let it go? When? — Now. What is released is released for good.

FAQ

常见问题Frequently asked

释放法和冥想有什么区别？How is releasing different from meditation?
冥想通常是观察念头、训练专注，多在当下带来平静；释放法更进一步——它不只处理眼前的情绪，还会释放情绪底下的欲望、限制性信念与内在阻力，放掉的部分不会再回来。两者不冲突，很多人会同时练习。Meditation observes thoughts and trains attention, bringing calm in the moment. Releasing goes further — it works on the wants, limiting beliefs, and inner resistance beneath a feeling, and what's released doesn't come back. Many people practice both.
